No admired site does all four, so the job is assembly. The parts to assemble are shown below as rendered mockups rather than described.

Colour

Palettes, rendered instead of quoted

Each row is a site's real colours, pulled from its stylesheet, with a sample paragraph set in them so you can see how the site reads. The pattern across the good ones: a tinted ground rather than black, two or three text brightnesses, the brightest kept off pure white, and at most one accent.
