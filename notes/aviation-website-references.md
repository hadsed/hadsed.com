# Aviation website motion references

October 4, 2026. Search for websites using cinematic fighter-jet shots, to complement the [film and flight references](jet-shot-references.md). This shortlist found adjacent aircraft experiences rather than a verified site reproducing our SR-71 distance-to-close storm shot. Do not treat a visually ambitious aerospace homepage as proof of that exact camera move.

## Northvane — closest aircraft camera study found

[Live experience](https://northvane-aero.vercel.app/) · [creator's description](https://contra.com/community/uLf65h6z-northvane-a-scroll-driven-3-d-story-where)

Browser checked: a rendered flying-wing aircraft occupies the foreground over a detailed mountainous landscape. Entering the exploration moves toward an overhead/specification view. The creator describes a fictional autonomous-aircraft brand, a sequence of scroll-triggered scenes, and camera easing. Useful for aircraft-to-terrain scale, resting camera positions, and how a web scene hands off to information. This is an autonomous aircraft concept, not a fighter jet or a storm-over-water shot. A sampled transition had clipping/viewport issues in our browser, so do not assume its implementation is ready to reuse.

## Jesko Jets — aviation landing and scroll reference

[Live website](https://jeskojets.com/) · [recorded flight-overlay interaction](https://60fps.design/appsites/jesko-jets-3d-flight-overlay-scroll-interaction)

Browser checked: the opening puts an aircraft cabin window and blue sky at the center of the landing composition. The recorded interaction source describes a scroll-controlled jet passing through the page. Useful for aviation imagery transitioning into readable site content; its luxury private-jet treatment is adjacent to our concept. The flight-overlay progression was not independently reproduced during the brief live check.

## ATMOS by LEEROY — cloud-space lead

[Live experiment](https://atmos.leeroy.ca/) · [showcase description](https://www.webgpu.com/showcase/atmos-scroll-driven-flight-procedural-skies/)

The live entry screen loaded in our browser, but the flight sequence did not advance during the brief check. The showcase describes a plane following a curved path through instanced clouds, with depth cues and scroll response. Keep this as a cloud-depth and interaction lead, not a verified fighter-jet cinematic reference. Its bright stylized palette is incidental to the spatial idea.

## Praetorian Aeronautics — military presentation lead

[Live website](https://praetorian-aeronautics.com/)

The current page identifies autonomous aircraft/interceptors and interactive system controls. It is a military aerospace presentation reference; an exact fighter-jet fly-in sequence has not been verified. Useful to compare restrained dark presentation and aircraft inspection with our entirely wordless introduction.

## Dassault / Studio River — cinematic aviation films embedded in a web project

[FOCUS ON project description](https://www.studioriver.fr/portfolio/focus-on/)

The studio describes an immersive aviation-film series for Dassault, including Rafale details, in a website section. A primary-source lead for edited aircraft cinematography and lighting; the films themselves were not visually checked in this search. This is a film-on-web reference rather than a confirmed continuous interactive camera path.

## Decision for the next test

The cheaper design decision is to compare our existing timed camera brief with a poster-only generation before building more geometry. The website references help with eventual browser presentation, but they do not supply a verified replacement for the chosen shot. Keep the camera research and visual styling as separate decisions.
